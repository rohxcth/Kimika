# Style anchor: M&A deal snapshot and process sections

The following examples show the expected prose density, table format, and tone for M&A deal reports. Use these as the benchmark for structure and register.

---

## Example: Deal snapshot table

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Acquirer | Westpac Banking Corporation (ASX: WBC) |
| Target | Bank of Melbourne Financial Group (ASX: BMG) |
| Structure | Scheme of arrangement |
| Consideration | A$6.80 cash per share |
| Premium | 32.4% to BMG's undisturbed close of A$5.13 on 14 Oct 2026; 28.7% to 30-day VWAP of A$5.28 |
| Implied equity value | A$3.2bn |
| EV | A$3.6bn (including A$0.4bn net debt per SID) |
| EV/EBITDA | 9.2x (FY2026 consensus EBITDA A$391m) |
| Conditions | APRA approval; ACCC clearance; BMG shareholder approval (75% of votes cast); Federal Court sanction |
| Status | SID signed 24 Oct 2026; scheme booklet expected Feb 2027 |
| Expected close | Q2 2027 |
| BMG advisers | Goldman Sachs (financial); Herbert Smith Freehills (legal) |
| WBC advisers | UBS (financial); Allens (legal) |
| Break fee | A$32m (1.0% of equity value), payable by BMG in specified circumstances |

Westpac is acquiring 100% of BMG's issued capital by way of a court-approved scheme. BMG's board recommends the scheme unanimously, in the absence of a superior proposal and subject to the independent expert concluding the scheme is in the best interests of BMG shareholders.

## Example: Process and negotiations section

BMG first received an unsolicited, non-binding indicative proposal from Westpac on 3 Sep 2026 at A$6.20 per share (21.1% premium to the then-undisturbed price of A$5.12). BMG's board considered the proposal inadequate and declined to grant due diligence.

Westpac submitted a revised proposal of A$6.55 on 18 Sep 2026, following which BMG granted four weeks of exclusive due diligence. During that period, a second potential acquirer submitted a preliminary expression of interest at approximately A$6.40; BMG did not engage, citing the exclusivity obligations.

A final proposal of A$6.80 was submitted on 21 Oct 2026. BMG's board concluded that A$6.80 was compelling relative to the independent expert's assessed value range and recommended the scheme on 24 Oct 2026 when the SID was executed.
