## Example: Valuation section

BMG's independent expert, Grant Samuel, assessed the full underlying value of BMG shares at A$5.90 to A$7.10, placing the A$6.80 offer within the assessed range. Grant Samuel concluded the scheme is fair and reasonable and in the best interests of BMG shareholders in the absence of a superior proposal.

**Valuation methods and ranges**

| Method | Low | High | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| DCF | A$5.70 | A$7.30 | WACC 9.5-10.5%; terminal growth 2.0-2.5% |
| Trading comparables (EV/EBITDA) | A$5.40 | A$6.60 | 8.0-9.5x applied to FY2026E EBITDA; 15% control premium added |
| Precedent transactions | A$6.20 | A$7.80 | 8 selected AU financial services transactions, 2018-2026; median 9.5x EV/EBITDA |
| **Offer price** | **A$6.80** | | 9.2x FY2026E EV/EBITDA |

The offer implies EV/EBITDA of 9.2x on FY2026 consensus EBITDA of A$391m, within the precedent transaction range of 8.4x-11.4x. The 32.4% premium to the undisturbed price exceeds the median 29% control premium in AU scheme transactions since 2020 (Grant Samuel analysis).

No synergy estimates have been publicly disclosed by either party. Westpac noted that integration of BMG's branch network and funding platform is expected to deliver cost efficiencies, but declined to quantify these.

*Peer comparison (Kimika calculation from public data):* The offer implies a P/E of 14.2x on FY2026 consensus earnings of A$224m, versus the ASX-listed regional bank peer median of 12.8x, suggesting a 10.9% premium to peer trading multiples on a P/E basis.
